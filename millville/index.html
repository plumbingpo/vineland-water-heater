Keeping Your Millville Water Heater Running Longer

Most early failures trace back to maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Drain and flush the tank once a year to clear sediment, get the anode rod checked every few years, and operate the relief valve annually so it doesn't seize. Set the thermostat close to 120 degrees to reduce scald risk and slow scale buildup. For a tankless heater in a hard-water area, plan on descaling once a year so the heat exchanger stays clean.

The age of your heater matters just as much. With regular service, a tank water heater usually runs 8 to 12 years, a heat pump water heater 10 to 15, and a tankless unit up to 20. Once a heater passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like discolored hot water, rumbling, or a damp spot under the tank, it's smart to start planning a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule is cheaper and is far less stressful than scrambling for a new heater with water spreading across the floor.

Minor issues don't stay minor for long. Take a spent anode rod: replacing it is a short, affordable repair, but if nobody replaces it, the water starts eating the tank steel, and just like that a minor fix has become a full replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to keep our number in their phone and reach out at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and spares you a surprise cleanup.

Common Water Heater Problems in Millville

In Millville, we work on every kind of water heater, and each one has a typical way of failing. Gas tank heaters usually give trouble through thermocouples and flame sensors, dirty burners, and draft problems in the flue. Electric tanks lose elements and thermostats, especially when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless units throw error codes for scale in the heat exchanger, ignition failures, and flow sensor trouble, and many of those trace back to skipped descaling. Heat pump water heaters need clean air filters and a clear condensate drain, and they struggle in cramped closets.
